Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Landoulsi Technologies FZE LLC, trading as Pluminate ("we", "us"), operates this website and publishes PlumTerm. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

PlumTerm

PlumTerm currently collects no analytics, usage data or crash reports, and does not send your commands, files or terminal output to us. If this ever changes, it will be optional and described here first.

Some optional features contact third-party services at your request, for example the AI commands and pull request lookups through the GitHub CLI. Those services have their own privacy policies.

This website

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated first. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical information such as IP addresses to serve the pages.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your name, email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to respond to you and to meet our legal and record-keeping obligations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing plumterm@pluminate.com.
